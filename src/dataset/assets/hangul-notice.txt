KOSDIC Hangul single-line font (KOSDICHS)

Source: https://github.com/golanlevin/p5-single-line-font-resources
(hangeul_monoline_fonts: AutoCAD SHX fonts converted to SVG 1.1 fonts)

Original font: "Hangul Hnaja Special character Simplex font for KOSDIC",
designed by Choi Samrak (2006). The KOSDIC vector fonts are published by the
Korea Institute of Construction Technology, which states that anyone may use
them without restriction.

The Hangul stroke data cached by AutoQV is converted from that SVG font into
a different format (normalized JSON polylines). Syllables that are not among
the original font's 2,350 precomposed glyphs were assembled from jamo by the
upstream converter.
